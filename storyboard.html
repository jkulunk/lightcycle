TRON: LIGHT CYCLE — STORYBOARD & DESIGN

PLAYER (CYAN) VS. COMPUTER AI (ORANGE) // 3 CAMERA MODES

[0] Intro: Handheld Rod Initialization

  • Stage 1 (0.0–0.8s): Riders ignite handheld batons at chest height
  • Stage 2 (0.8–1.8s): Forward leap; laser wheels & wireframe canopy rez around rod
  • Stage 3 (1.8–2.8s): Cycles slam onto the Grid & camera interpolates to chosen view

[1] Bird's-Eye View (Top-Down)

  • Camera: High-altitude Orthographic / low-FOV overhead at Y = 180
  • Controls: Cardinal (W/A/S/D) or Relative (A/D)
  • Strength: Complete arena & Voronoi territory visibility

[2] Over-the-Shoulder (Chase Cam)

  • Camera: Perspective FOV 65°, 8u behind & 3.5u above cycle
  • Dynamics: 120ms spring-damped yaw slerp on 90° turns + bike lean
  • Strength: Cinematic sense of speed and 3D light-wall height

[3] First-Person (Cockpit Cam)

  • Camera: Perspective FOV 85° inside translucent cycle canopy
  • HUD Aids: Top-right 2D Radar Minimap + forward wall proximity warning
  • Strength: Maximum immersion and high-speed reflex intensity

Computer AI ("Sark / Clu" Heuristics)

  • Survival Lookahead: Raycasts Forward, Left, and Right at each grid intersection
  • BFS Flood-Fill: Counts reachable open cells to avoid self-trapping boxes
  • Cutoff Mode: Detects parallel player vectors and turns across to cut them off
